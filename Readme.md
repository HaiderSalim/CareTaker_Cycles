# Caretaker's Cycle

**Theme:** Cyber Garden  
**Platform:** Unity 3D (Windows Build)  
**Team:** Haider Salim (UI, player controller, scene setup, compilation), Asad Ali (Puzzles, custom assets)

---

## Concept
In a distant future, Earth has been abandoned by humans. In their absence, machines and nature have evolved into a symbiotic ecosystem. As a visiting alien caretaker, you arrive periodically to ensure this fragile balance is preserved. Explore the post-human world, uncover remnants of civilization, and restore harmony through puzzle-solving.

### Key Features
- **Puzzle 1**: Reconnect broken circuits by wiring bulbs and a generator.
- **Puzzle 2**: Restore environmental balance by planting two trees in designated zones.
- **Narrative Discovery**: Small environmental clues and objects hint at humanity's past.
- **Unique Mechanic**: Player must actively balance nature and tech elements to progress.

---

## Controls
- **WASD**: Move
- **Mouse**: Look around
- **Left Click**: Interact / Shoot (context-sensitive)

---

## Development Process
Caretaker's Cycle was developed in 7 days for a game jam under the theme "Cyber Garden."
- We focused on creating harmony between machine and nature via visuals and puzzle design.
- Puzzle logic was implemented through Unity's interaction system.
- UI, player movement, and core gameplay flow were built using Unity’s built-in tools.
- The environment combines both natural (trees, plants) and artificial (wires, generator) assets.
